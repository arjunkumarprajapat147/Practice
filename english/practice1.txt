RULE: "I am" vs "I have"
  I am  = main hoon        ->  I am a student.
  I have = mere paas hai   ->  I have a phone.

EXAMPLES
  I am Arjun.
  I am from Rajasthan.
  I have a laptop.
  I have two brothers.

PRACTICE - apne baare mein 3 line English mein likho:
1. My name is ____________________
2. I am a ________ student at ____________________
3. I live in ____________________

BONUS - "I am" ya "I have" bharo:
a) ____ hungry.
b) ____ a bike.
c) ____ 20 years old.
d) ____ an exam tomorrow.
